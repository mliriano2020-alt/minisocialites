About Mini Socialites

We believe every child deserves spaces where they are welcomed, celebrated, and free to be themselves—without barriers or judgment.

Our Mission

Mini Socialites exists to create joyful, inclusive social experiences for children with disabilities and their families, fostering connection, confidence, and community.


Our Story

Mini Socialites began with a simple idea: families of children with disabilities shouldn’t feel alone or excluded from everyday social experiences. What started as a few small gatherings has grown into a community of families, volunteers, and partners who believe in the power of shared joy.

Who We Serve

We serve children with disabilities and their families, regardless of diagnosis, background, or income. Our events are designed to be:

  • Accessible and sensory‑aware
  • Judgment‑free and welcoming
  • Family‑centered and community‑driven

Our Values

  • Inclusion: Every child and family belongs.
  • Joy: Fun is essential, not optional.
  • Community: We are stronger together.
  • Respect: We honor each family’s unique story.

What We Do

Our programs are built around connection—between kids, caregivers, and the wider community.

Social Outings

Inclusive Events

We host regular social outings tailored to the needs of children with disabilities and their families—playdates, park meetups, museum visits, sensory‑friendly events, and more.

Family Support

Connection for Caregivers

Our gatherings create space for caregivers to connect, share experiences, and build friendships with others who understand their journey.

Community Building

Partnerships & Inclusion

We collaborate with local businesses, schools, and organizations to create more inclusive spaces and expand opportunities for families.
